The region

Discover inland Portugal

Casa da Tapada is the perfect base to explore the nature, villages and traditions of the Beira Interior region.

Historic Villages

Castelo Novo, Monsanto and Alpedrinha, with their stone streets and centuries of history.

Mountains

Trails and viewpoints in the Gardunha range and, a day trip away, the Serra da Estrela.

Festivals and flavours

The Santo António festivities, the sausage and ham fair and the famous Fundão cherries.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I book?

Send us a message by WhatsApp or email with your dates and number of guests. We will confirm availability and the price, and your booking is secured with a 50% payment. See all the details on the Book page.

How many people can the house sleep?

Up to 6 people: two bedrooms with double beds and a sofa bed in the living room.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, pets are welcome by prior arrangement.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 3 pm and check-out by 11 am. Other times can be arranged depending on availability.

Is the pool available all year round?

The pool is private and for guests only. The water is not heated, so using it depends on the weather and on your own preference: there is no fixed season.

Is there Wi-Fi and parking?

Yes. There is Wi-Fi in the house and outdoor parking next to the house.

Is the house suitable for children and babies?

Yes, children and babies are welcome. We provide a baby high chair, but no cot. The pool is not fenced, so children must always be supervised by an adult.

Is smoking allowed?

Smoking is not allowed inside the house. You are welcome to smoke outdoors.
