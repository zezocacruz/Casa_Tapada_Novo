The house

Every corner designed for you

A detached country house for up to 6 guests, with a private pool and outdoor space, lovingly cared for by the family.

Bedrooms Living room Kitchen and dining area Bathroom Pool Barbecue and outdoor spaces Amenities

Bedrooms

Two bedrooms with double beds, in soft tones, with bed linen and towels included, for truly restful nights.

Living room

A cosy living room with a stone fireplace, air conditioning and a sofa bed for two more guests. The place for cold evenings and unhurried conversations.

Kitchen and dining area

A fully equipped kitchen and a dining table, open plan with the living room, for cooking and sharing meals together.

Bathroom

A bright, clean and practical bathroom with traditional tiles.

Pool

A private outdoor pool, for guests only, with sun loungers and views over the olive grove. The water is not heated.

Barbecue and outdoor spaces

Barbecue, a shaded table, a fire area, a hammock and a swing among the olive trees: an outdoor space made for long days and warm nights.
