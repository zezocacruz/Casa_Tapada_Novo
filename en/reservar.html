Booking conditions

Payment and confirmation
  • Your booking is confirmed upon payment of 50% of the total price of the stay.
  • The remaining 50% and the security deposit are paid up to 7 days before check-in.
  • Failure to pay within this period results in the automatic cancellation of the booking.
  • We accept payment by MB WAY and bank transfer.
Cancellation
  • Cancellations up to 14 days before arrival: 50% of the booking amount is retained.
  • Cancellations less than 14 days before arrival: non-refundable.
  • In case of no-show, the full price of the stay is due.
Security deposit
  • A security deposit of €150, paid together with the remaining balance.
  • It is refunded after the property has been checked, within 3 days of check-out.
Times
  • Check-in from 3 pm and check-out by 11 am.
  • Requests for different times may be considered, depending on availability.
House rules
  • Maximum capacity of 6 guests.
  • No smoking inside the house. Smoking is allowed in outdoor areas.
  • Pets allowed by prior arrangement.
  • Parties and events only with the owners' prior authorisation.
  • Please take care of the equipment, furniture and outdoor spaces.
Children and pool
  • Children and babies are welcome. We provide a baby high chair, but no cot.
  • The pool is not heated and has no safety fence: children must always be supervised by an adult.
Check-in and documents
  • At check-in we ask for an identity document for each guest, as required by Portuguese law for guest registration.

By booking, you accept these conditions and the Terms and Conditions.
