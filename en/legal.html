These terms apply to bookings and stays at Casa da Tapada. By confirming a booking, the guest declares that they have read and accept these terms and the conditions published on the Book page (prices, payment, cancellation, deposit, times and house rules), which form part of this agreement.

Booking and price

Booking requests can be made by WhatsApp, phone, email or social media. A booking is only confirmed after the payment set out in the booking conditions. Prices are per night, for the whole house, and include applicable taxes. No municipal tourist tax is charged.

Guest obligations

The guest agrees to use the house and its contents with care, to respect the maximum number of guests agreed, the house rules and the neighbours' rest, and to present an identity document for each guest at check-in, as required by law.

The pool has no safety fence and is not supervised. Its use is the guests' responsibility, and children must always be supervised by an adult.

3. Privacy Policy

We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Portuguese law.

Data controller

Leontino Costa Pinto, owner of Casa da Tapada. Contact for privacy matters: casadatapadaturismo@gmail.com.

What data we process

This website has no forms or user accounts and does not collect personal data directly. We only process the data you send us when you contact us or book: name, contact details, dates of stay, number of guests, special requests, billing details (including tax number, if you request an invoice) and the identity document details requested at check-in.

Purposes and legal basis

  • Replying to enquiries and managing the booking and stay (performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps).
  • Issuing invoices and complying with tax obligations (legal obligation).
  • Guest registration and the mandatory reporting of foreign guests to the competent authorities (legal obligation).

Who we share data with

Only with those required by law (for example, the Portuguese Tax Authority and the authorities responsible for guest registration). The messages you send us go through the services you choose to use (WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, email, Airbnb), which have their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share data for advertising.

How long we keep data

We keep data only for as long as needed for the booking and stay and, after that, for the periods required by law (for example, billing documents are kept for the legal tax retention period).

Your rights

You may request access to, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, and object to its processing, by writing to the email above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority, CNPD (www.cnpd.pt).
